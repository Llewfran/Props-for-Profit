# Props for Profit — Consolidated Rules (v2)

*Fly. Haul. Hustle.*

This version merges the original Rules doc, Player Reference Sides A & B, the company cards and the designer's rulings of 21–22 Nov 2026, and adds the Advanced Options and a Quick Reference. Items marked **[TBD]** are still open.

---

## Components

- 18 Map Cards (A–T, no I or O): 2 Mainland (A Aurelia City, B Breakwater Bay) + 16 grid cards
- 21 Location Cards: A×1, B×1, C×4, D×2, E×2, F×1, G×1, H×2, J×1, K×1, L×1, M×1, N×2, P×1
- 75 Demand Cards, each with a compass letter (N/E/S/W): Pax 2×8, Pax 4×16, Pax 6×10, Pax 8×4, Pax 10×8, Pax 12×4, Freight 1×12, Freight 2×12, Freight 3×1
- 2 Permanent Demand Cards (no compass) with coloured backs: Pax 4 (Charter 6) and Cargo 3 (Charter 7)
- 7 Charter Required Cards (1–7) and 3 row signs: Departure, Arrival, Lounge / Freight
- 36 Plane Cards (show Ref #, Type, Pax # and/or Freight #, Range and value; engines are counted from the artwork). Five are Combi Planes (#32–#36).
- 6 Company Cards with matching Flight Schedule sets (Charter Accepted 1–7, Aircraft Booked 1–4)
- Per player colour: Plane marker, numbered markers 1–4, Tarmac Cards 1–4 (Tarmac 4 is bought during play)
- Storm (Cloud) marker, Map Compass card, Turn Counter card with a white turn marker
- High Maintenance cards, 50% Discount (Second Hand) pool sign, ERI cards
- Money: $1, $5, $25, $100

**Players:** 6 (one per airline).

## Setup

### 1. Build the Island Map
Shuffle the 16 non-mainland Map Cards and lay them face-up in a random **4 × 4 grid**. Place the Mainland cards immediately west of the grid: **A – Aurelia City** beside Row 2, **B – Breakwater Bay** beside Row 3.

Place the **Map Compass** in the space north of Aurelia City (beside Row 1) and the **Turn Counter** in the space south of Breakwater Bay (beside Row 4). Put the **white turn marker** on space **1** of the Turn Counter.

### 2. Map Distances
Each Map Card shows an **East** value (right edge) and a **South** value (bottom edge). These are the distance across that card boundary and apply in both directions (East value for east/west crossings, South value for north/south crossings). Add the values of every boundary crossed to get the Distance.

**Mainland:** Aurelia City is always directly north of Breakwater Bay. Aurelia City's **South** value is the distance between the two Mainland cities.
**Plane access:** the icons at the top of each card show which aircraft may land there — H-L (Helo-Land), H-F (Helo-Float), FW-L (Fixed Wing-Land), FW-F (Fixed Wing-Float). Cards with no icons (open sea: Q Queen's Deep, R Racing Pods, S Sapphire Sea, T Tradewind Sea) may be flown over but never landed on.

### 3. Place the Cloud
Place the Cloud marker on **T – Tradewind Sea**. No Plane may land on, or fly through, the Cloud's location. A Plane already at the Cloud's location **may take off** from it.

### 4. Build the Charter Board
Place the seven Charter Required cards face-up, left to right: **1 2 3 4 5 6 7**. To their left, place the row signs top to bottom: **Departure, Arrival, Lounge / Freight**. This makes a 7-column × 3-row board.

**Permanent Charters (6 & 7)** — constant demand between the Mainland and the islands; these never change:

|  | Charter 6 | Charter 7 |
|---|---|---|
| Departure | A – Aurelia City | B – Breakwater Bay |
| Arrival | C – Crown Island | C – Crown Island |
| Lounge / Freight | Pax 4 | Cargo 3 |

**Variable Charters (1–5):**
1. Shuffle the Location deck face-down. Draw 5 and place one in each Departure space, Charter 1 → 5.
2. Draw 5 more and place one in each Arrival space, Charter 1 → 5.
   - If an Arrival matches the Departure in its column, place the matching card at the bottom of the Location deck and draw again from the top.
3. Choose game length by using **55, 65 or 75 Demand cards** (for 55 or 65, shuffle all 75 and take that many at random). Shuffle them face-down as the Demand deck. Draw 5 and place one face-up in each Lounge / Freight space, Charter 1 → 5.

### 5. Assign the Airlines
Shuffle the Company Cards and deal one to each player. Each player takes the matching set of 12 Flight Schedule Cards (Charter Accepted 1–7, Aircraft Booked 1–4).

| Airline | Special Ability |
|---|---|
| Plane & Simple Charters | $15 discount on the first **Single** purchased |
| On a Wing and a Prayer Airways | Pays **$5 per engine** for Maintenance (50% off) |
| Hope Floats Aviation | $15 discount on the first **Float Plane** purchased |
| Against All Odds Aviation | $30 discount on the first **Twin** purchased |
| Plane Lucky Charter | $15 discount on the first **Helo** purchased |
| Rust Bucket Airline | $3 discount on **each Cargo Plane** purchased |

**Discounts apply to full-price purchases only.** A company discount applies only to a new Plane bought at its full card price (in the draft or from the Plane deck). "First" means the first such full-price purchase of that kind of Plane. A Plane bought from the 50% Discount Pool never gets a company discount and does not use up a "first" discount.

> Card correction needed: the On a Wing and a Prayer card currently reads "Pay only $1 per engine for all services" and must be reprinted.

### Aircraft Terms
- **Plane** means any aircraft: fixed wing and helicopter alike. Every rule that says "Plane" applies to Helos too.
- **Engines** are counted from the artwork on the Plane card (no engine number is printed).
  - **Exception:** Plane **#6** (the $55 land helicopter: H-L, Freight 2, Range 5) has **2 engines**.
- Every Plane card shows a unique **Ref #** in its top-left corner (see the Aircraft List).
- **Single** = any Plane with 1 engine; **Twin** = any Plane with 2 engines, including helicopters.
- **Cargo Plane** = any Plane with a Freight (pallet) value, including helicopters.
- **Float Plane** = any Plane with a float type (FW-F or H-F), including float helicopters.
- **Combi Plane** = Planes #32, #33, #34, #35 and #36, which show both a Pax and a Freight value. When booked on a Charter, a Combi Plane carries **either** Pax **or** Freight (not both), using the matching capacity and Cargo Type Factor. Combi Planes count as Cargo Planes.

These definitions apply to the Company Special Abilities.

### 6. Player Colour
Each player takes a colour set: Plane marker (placed on their Company Card), numbered markers 1–4 and Tarmac Cards 1–4. Lay Tarmac Cards 1–3 face-up in order (keep Tarmac 4 and marker 4 aside until bought), with each numbered marker on its Tarmac Card.

**The Tarmac number is the Plane's number.** When you own a Plane, its card sits partly over its Tarmac Card so the Tarmac number stays visible. That number is used both as the Plane's **Aircraft Booked** number and as the number of your coloured token on the map, which shows where the Plane is.

### 7. Starting Money
Each player receives **$100** from the Bank.

## Turn Sequence

**1 Procurement → 2 Scheduling → 3 Reposition → 4 Scheduled Flights → 5 Maintenance (every 4th turn) → 6 Charter Update + Weather → 7 Aircraft Sale**

### Step 1 — Procurement
- **First turn only:** each player draws 4 Plane cards, keeps 1 and passes the rest left; repeat until all 4 are drafted. Buy any drafted Planes you want and can afford (apply your Special Ability). Shuffle unpurchased Planes back into the Plane deck.
- **Later turns:** you may draw the top 2 Plane cards. You **may** buy 1 of them (buying is optional). Return **all** unbought cards to the bottom of the Plane deck.
- **Poorest airline (turns 2 onwards):** if one airline has **less cash than every other airline** (no tie) at the start of Step 1, it may instead draw the top **3** Plane cards and buy **up to 2** of them. All unbought cards go to the bottom of the Plane deck. The Tarmac limit and the ERI rule still apply.
- **50% Discount Pool:** you may buy a Plane from the pool for ½ its indicated value, rounded **UP** to the nearest $5. It receives a **High Maintenance** card. Company discounts do not apply to pool purchases, and a pool purchase does not count as your "first" Plane of any kind.
- **All airlines procure at the same time.** Buying is never compulsory.
- **Pool priority:** if two or more airlines want the same Plane from the 50% Discount Pool, the airline with the **lowest cash balance** buys it. If cash is tied, the airline with the fewest engines buys it; if still tied, draw lots.
- **Extra Tarmac:** in Step 1 of any turn, an airline may buy **Tarmac 4** for **$100** (once per game). It lets the airline own a fourth Plane. Buying it does not stop you buying a Plane in the same Step 1. No buying with an outstanding ERI.
- Place a new Plane card on the first available Tarmac Card (maximum 3 Planes, or 4 with Tarmac 4), **partly overlapping it so the Tarmac number stays visible**. Move the coloured token with that number to **C – Crown Island**, even if the Cloud is there (the Plane may take off from under the Cloud).
- A player with an outstanding ERI cannot purchase Planes.

### Step 2 — Scheduling
All players plan **simultaneously and secretly**. For each Charter you want to fly, place face-down a **Charter Accepted #** card paired with an **Aircraft Booked #** card (= Tarmac # / marker #).
- **Each Plane may fly only one Charter per turn.**
- **Each airline may book only one Plane per Charter** (one Charter Accepted card takes one Aircraft Booked card). The Demand Factor allocates a share of the demand to each airline; it is not a passenger count.
- **Exception — Connecting Flight:** an airline may place **two** Aircraft Booked cards on one Charter Accepted card, but only to connect a flight (see Connecting Flights below).
- Consider Demand, Departure, Arrival, location compatibility, current Plane position, Range and capacity.
- Do not continue until every player has finished.

### Step 3 — Reposition
Players may move Planes (e.g. to a Departure location): announce the Plane and destination, others check Range and compatibility, move the marker, pay Operating Expense. **Repositioning earns no income.**

### Step 4 — Scheduled Flights
Resolve Charters strictly in order **1 → 7**. For each Charter:
1. **Reveal** the scheduled operators and booked Planes.
2. **Cancel?** Any operator may cancel for a **$5** penalty (a mandatory payment).
3. **Demand Factor** for each remaining operator:
   - Start with the printed Demand. If the column holds **two Demand cards of the same service, add them together** (Freight 1 + Freight 3 = Freight 4; Pax 4 + Pax 6 = Pax 10).
   - Deduct 1 for each competing airline's aircraft.
   - **Pax only — comfort modifiers**, applied only when your service is genuinely better than a competitor's: More engines **+1**; Non-stop instead of hopping **+1**. Equal service = no modifier.
   - **Freight: no modifiers** (engines, speed and non-stop give no advantage).
   - Minimum 1; maximum printed Demand; **cannot exceed the Plane's capacity** (Pax # or Freight #).
4. **Income = Demand Factor × (Plane Type Factor + Cargo Type Factor) × Charter Distance**

   | Service | Fixed Wing | Helo |
   |---|---|---|
   | Pax (+$0) | $2 | $3 |
   | Freight (+$2) | $4 | $5 |

   **Charter Distance** is the shortest distance between the Departure and Arrival locations, ignoring every other factor (Cloud, Plane Range, location compatibility and hops). Income always uses Charter Distance; Operating Expense uses the distance actually flown.
5. **Fly:** receive income, move the Plane's marker to the Arrival location, pay Operating Expense.
6. **Fulfilment:** a Charter is fulfilled when **one or more** scheduled flights respond to it. Turn its Demand card face-down. If a Charter shows both a Pax and a Freight Demand card, fulfilling either one closes the Charter.

Finish every flight on a Charter before moving to the next.

### Connecting Flights
An airline may fly one Charter with two of its own Planes that connect at an intermediate airport.
- **Booking:** one Charter Accepted card with two Aircraft Booked cards.
- **Plane 1** flies the load from the Departure to the **connecting airport**. **Plane 2** flies it from the connecting airport to the Arrival.
- Plane 2 must be **at the connecting airport at the start of Step 4** (it may reposition there in Step 3).
- Both Planes must carry the same service (Pax or Freight; a Combi Plane may carry either), and the connecting airport must be compatible with both Planes.
- The pair counts as **one competing airline** and as a **hopping** service (no non-stop modifier).
- Income uses the **lower** Plane Type Factor of the two Planes, and the Demand Factor cannot exceed the **smaller** capacity.
- Each Plane pays its own Operating Expense. Plane 1 ends at the connecting airport; Plane 2 ends at the Arrival.

### Impossible Direct Bonus
A Charter is an **Impossible Direct** when no Plane type that can carry its demand can land at both the Departure and the Arrival (check the access icons).
An airline that completes an Impossible Direct with a Connecting Flight uses **Charter Distance = the actual distance flown** (Departure → connecting airport → Arrival, not counting repositioning). No other modifier applies.
- If more than one airline completes it, all of them use the **shortest** distance flown by a successful airline.

### Operating Expense (reposition and scheduled flights)
**(# Engines × Maintenance Factor × Distance actually flown) + $1 per Landing**

| Plane Type | Normal | High Maintenance |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed Wing (FW) | 1 | 2 |
| Helo (H) | 2 | 3 |

High Maintenance = normal factor +1.

### Range and Hopping
- **Range is the limit per leg.** A Plane may reach a destination beyond its Range by **hopping**: landing at intermediate locations, each leg within Range.
- Every landing costs $1, including intermediate stops and the final Arrival.
- Departure, Arrival and every intermediate stop must be compatible with the Plane (access icons). Open-sea cards cannot be used as stops.
- No Plane may fly through or land on the Cloud's location. A Plane at the Cloud's location may take off.
- **Planes stay where they land.**

### Step 5 — Maintenance
When the white turn marker is on space **4** of the Turn Counter (**turns 4, 8, 12, 16…**), each player pays **$10 × every engine in their fleet** (On a Wing and a Prayer: $5 per engine). This is in addition to Operating Expenses. Skip on all other turns.

### Step 6 — Charter Update
Variable Charters **1–5 only**, working left → right.

For each column:
- **Fulfilled** (any Demand card face-down): clear the whole Charter.
- **Not fulfilled, one Demand card:** add a **second Demand card** from the Demand deck to the column. The Charter stays. If both cards are the **same service, their demands add up** (Freight 1 + Freight 3 = Freight 4), so a Charter that waits a turn becomes more attractive. If one is Pax and one Freight, the Charter offers both and flying either fulfils it.
- **Not fulfilled, already two Demand cards:** clear the whole Charter.

Clearing: Location cards go to the Location discard pile; Demand cards go to the Demand discard pile.

Refill cleared columns, left → right:
1. Refill empty Departures from the Location deck.
2. Refill empty Arrivals. If an Arrival matches its Departure, put that card on the bottom of the Location deck and draw again from the top.
3. Draw one new Demand card for each refreshed column.
4. **When the last Location card is drawn**, immediately shuffle the Location discard pile to form a new Location deck.

**Charters 6 and 7 never change.**

**Weather (without the app):** the compass letter on the **first** new Demand card drawn this step (including a second Demand card added to an unfulfilled column) moves the Cloud **one card** in that direction. Only the first card moves the Cloud. If the Cloud would leave the 4 × 4 grid, it wraps to the opposite edge.

**Weather (with the app):** the app moves the Cloud one card along the path fixed by the Game Code; ignore the compass on the Demand cards.

### Step 7 — Aircraft Sale
**No Planes may be sold or scrapped in the final turn.**

- **Sell (normal Plane):** sell to the 50% Discount Pool for ½ its indicated value, rounded **DOWN** to the nearest $5. Place it **face-up** in the pool. Its Tarmac becomes available.
- **High Maintenance Planes cannot be resold.** They may be **scrapped** for $0: remove the Plane and its High Maintenance card from the game; the Tarmac becomes available.
- **ERI repayment** may be made in this step (see below).
- **Finally, move the white turn marker** one space on the Turn Counter (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1).

## Emergency Recovery Injection (ERI)
- **Trigger:** if you cannot meet a mandatory payment, you immediately receive **$100** from the Bank of Pacifica and take an ERI card. You now owe **$120**.
- While the ERI is outstanding: you cannot purchase Planes; you cannot receive a second ERI; you may otherwise operate normally (fly, reposition, sell, scrap).
- Failing another mandatory payment while holding an ERI = **bankruptcy and elimination**.
- **Repayment:** $120 in full, only during Step 7, optional. You may buy Planes again from the next turn.
- An airline with an outstanding ERI at game end **cannot win**.

---

## Game End
When the last Demand card is allocated to the Charter Board during Step 6, finish that turn; the **following turn is the final turn**. If the Demand deck runs out before all columns can be refilled, leave those spaces empty. Play the final turn normally (Steps 1–7, but no Planes may be sold); then the game ends.

## Winning
**Final score = your money + the value of the Planes you still own.** Each Plane counts **$5 for every full $20 of its printed price** (25%, rounded down to $5): under $20 = $0 · $20–39 = $5 · $40–59 = $10 · $60–79 = $15 · $80–99 = $20 · $100–119 = $25, and so on. For example, a $70 Plane counts $15 and a $45 Plane counts $10. High Maintenance Planes count $0. The highest score wins. Tie: most engines in the fleet wins. An airline with an outstanding ERI cannot win.

## Advanced Options

Once you know the standard game, add the Advanced Options: two new aircraft, seven **Headline** cards and five **Opportunity** cards. Nothing new is drawn: every event arrives on a card players already draw. All other rules are unchanged.

### Advanced setup
| Cards | How many | Goes into |
|---|---|---|
| #37 Amphibian, #38 Flying Boat | 2 | Plane deck |
| Opportunity cards (O-1 to O-5) | 5 | Plane deck |
| Headline cards (A1 to A7) | 7 | Demand deck |

1. **Plane deck:** shuffle in #37, #38 and the five Opportunity cards (43 cards).
2. **Demand deck:** make the deck at the chosen size (55, 65 or 75), then **remove 7 random cards** without looking at them and shuffle in the **7 Headlines**. The deck size is unchanged.
3. **Turn 1 draft:** an Opportunity card dealt into a draft hand is set aside and replaced. After the draft, shuffle the set-aside cards and the returned Planes back into the Plane deck.
4. **App:** choose **Advanced Options** on the New Game or Join screen; every player must choose the same.

### New aircraft
| Ref # | Name | Type | Pax | Freight | Range | Engines | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 37 | Amphibian "Goose" | FW-L + FW-F | 8 | – | 5 | 2 | $165 |
| 38 | Flying Boat | FW-F | 8 | 2 | 6 | 2 | $165 |

- **#37 Amphibian** lands wherever FW-L or FW-F Planes can (it cannot use helicopter-only locations). Twin and Float Plane.
- **#38 Flying Boat:**
  - **Open-water hop:** may land and refuel on an open-sea card (Q, R, S, T) in the middle of a route; never under the Cloud, and never to start or end a Charter. Each sea landing costs the normal landing fee.
  - **Demand Factor bonus:** on Pax, ignore the **first** rival airline. Freight competition is normal.
  - **Pax AND Freight together:** on a Charter with both a Pax and a Freight Demand card, it carries both on one flight and earns both incomes (each from its own card and rate). The Operating Expense is paid **once**.
  - Twin, Float Plane and Cargo Plane.
- In a **Connecting Flight**, either new Plane follows the normal Connecting Flight rules (no Flying Boat bonuses).

**Example:** Charter 3, Aurelia City → Driftwood (Charter Distance 11), shows Pax 6 and Freight 2; one rival flies Pax and one flies Freight. The Flying Boat hops A → R (sea, 5) → D (6). Expense 2 × 1 × 11 + $2 = $24. Pax: first rival ignored → Demand Factor 6 → 6 × $2 × 11 = $132. Freight: Demand Factor 1 → 1 × $4 × 11 = $44. Net **+$152**.

### Headline cards (Demand deck, Step 6)
A Headline is placed in its column like any Demand card, with its own Pax or Freight value, and can be flown like any Charter. Its effect starts when it is placed and lasts **while the card is displayed** (until its column is cleared, fulfilled or expired).

- **No compass:** without the app, if a Headline is the **first** new Demand card in Step 6, the Cloud **does not move** this turn. (With the app, the Cloud follows the Game Code path as usual.)
- **Effects add up:** Fuel Price Spike and Fuel Subsidy together cancel out.

| Card | Demand | Effect while displayed |
|---|---|---|
| Coronation Festival (A1) | Pax 3 | All Pax Demand Factors +1, including this Charter (capacity still limits). |
| Customs Crackdown (A2) | Freight 2 | Landings on Freight flights cost $3. |
| Government Mail Contract (A3) | Freight 2 | Every airline that flies this Charter with Freight 2 collects a flat $30; no Demand Factor, no competition. Flight costs still apply. |
| Fuel Price Spike (A4) | Freight 2 | Maintenance Factor +1 for every Plane on every flight. |
| Fuel Subsidy (A5) | Pax 6 | Maintenance Factor −1 for every Plane on every flight, minimum 0. |
| Royal Visit to Crown Island (A6) | Pax 10 | Charter 6 (A → C) is Pax 8. |
| Strike! (A7) | Pax 2 | Planes cannot reposition: skip Step 3. |

**Fuel cards** change the Maintenance Factor of every Plane (fixed wing and helicopter, High Maintenance included) for every flight, including repositioning. Scheduled Maintenance in Step 5 is **not** affected ($10 per engine; $5 for On a Wing and a Prayer).

| Maintenance Factor | Normal | Fuel Spike | Fuel Subsidy | Both |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed wing | 1 | 2 | 0 | 1 |
| Fixed wing, High Maintenance | 2 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| Helicopter | 2 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| Helicopter, High Maintenance | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 |

### Opportunity cards (Plane deck, Step 1)
In Step 1, an Opportunity card you draw may be taken **into your hand instead of procuring a Plane this turn**; otherwise it goes to the bottom of the deck with your other unbought cards. A used card leaves the game.

- **Kept:** Pay Out and Exclusive Contract stay in your hand until used. The others are played immediately.
- **ERI:** a player holding an ERI still draws in Step 1. They cannot buy a Plane, but may take an Opportunity card.

| Card | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Pay Out (O-1) | Keep | When you dispose of a High Maintenance Plane in Step 7 (normally scrapped for $0), receive $30. Not in the final turn. |
| Spare Part Sale (O-2) | Now | Pay $20 to remove the High Maintenance card from a Plane. It is "as new". |
| Exclusive Contract (O-3) | Keep | Play with your Charter Accepted card: on that Charter, Pax or Freight, rivals do not reduce your Demand Factor (capacity still limits). |
| Bank Promotion (O-4) | Now | Repay any ERI for only $100. |
| Retired Pilot Plane (O-5) | Now | Buy any Plane from the 50% pool at its pool price without taking a High Maintenance card. It is "as new". No company discount; not a "first" Plane; needs a free Tarmac. |

"As new" = normal costs, and the Plane may later be sold to the 50% pool.

### Advanced Options in the app
- **Switch:** Advanced Options on the New Game, Join or Fleet screen. The Rules tab then shows an Advanced Options summary.
- **Aircraft:** #37 and #38 appear in every plane picker. Routes for #38 may include a sea hop ("land & refuel"). For #38 choose Pax, Freight or **Both**; Both takes the two printed Demands and the rivals on each, applies the Pax bonus and counts the expense once.
- **Opportunity cards (Fleet screen):** mark Pay Out and Exclusive Contract as held. Pay Out offers "scrap #X for $30" on High Maintenance Planes. A held Exclusive Contract adds a "Play my Exclusive Contract" tick to the Flight and Connecting calculators. Spare Part Sale sets a Plane back to Normal; Bank Promotion and Retired Pilot Plane are recorded with one tap.
- **Headlines:** the calculators do not apply Headline effects; adjust the Maintenance Factor (Fuel), landing fee (Customs), Pax Demand Factor (Coronation) or income (Mail Contract) yourself.

## Quick Reference

### Standard game
| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 Procurement | Turn 1: draft 4. Later: draw 2, may buy 1 (outright poorest: draw 3, buy up to 2); unbought to the bottom. 50% pool: ½ price rounded UP + High Maintenance; lowest cash has priority. Tarmac 4 for $100 once. |
| 2 Scheduling | Secret: Charter Accepted # + Aircraft Booked # (Tarmac #). One Charter per Plane; one Plane per airline per Charter (two for a Connecting Flight). |
| 3 Reposition | Move Planes; pay expense; no income. |
| 4 Flights (1 → 7) | Reveal, cancel ($5)? Demand Factor = printed (same-service cards added) − 1 per rival (Pax: +1 more engines, +1 non-stop vs hopping); min 1, max printed, max capacity. Income = Demand Factor × Rate × Charter Distance. |
| 5 Maintenance | Marker on 4: $10 per engine ($5 On a Wing and a Prayer). |
| 6 Charter Update | Fulfilled → clear; 1 card unflown → add a 2nd (same service: the two add up); 2 cards unflown → clear. Refill. First new Demand card's compass moves the Cloud. |
| 7 Aircraft Sale | Sell ½ price rounded DOWN (not High Maintenance; scrap for $0). Repay ERI $120. No sales in the final turn. Move the turn marker. |

- **Rates:** Pax FW $2, Helo $3; Freight FW $4, Helo $5.
- **Expense:** engines × Maintenance Factor (FW 1, Helo 2, +1 High Maintenance) × distance flown + $1 per landing.
- **ERI:** +$100, owe $120; no buying; second failure = bankrupt; cannot win while outstanding.
- **End:** last Demand card placed → one final turn. Score = money + $5 for every full $20 of each Plane still owned (High Maintenance $0). Highest score wins; tie → most engines.
